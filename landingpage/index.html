Three steps. No student accounts.

A session takes seconds to open and closes itself when the timer ends.

Lecturer opens a session

Pick the unit, set a duration (5, 10, 15 minutes or custom) and a radius. The lecturer's GPS position is captured automatically.

Students check in

Share the link or QR code. Students enter their registration number and confirm their location from the browser.

Records build themselves

Watch check-ins arrive live, with distance per student. Export PDF or Excel reports when the session closes.

Seven checks stop proxy attendance.

Every check-in passes each layer before it is recorded as present.

Distance from the lecturer

The check-in must fall within the session radius, calculated from the lecturer's stored GPS position.

GPS accuracy gate

Weak or imprecise readings (worse than 200 m or the session radius) are rejected.

Device fingerprint

One browser or device cannot check in more than one student in the same session.

One check-in per student

Duplicate submissions are blocked, and a returning browser cannot resubmit.

Google account uniqueness

Where captured, each Google account can be used once per session.

Session expiry

The link stops working when the timer ends or the lecturer ends the session early.

Registered students only

The registration number must belong to a student enrolled in that unit.

Audit trail

Each check-in logs IP, device, browser and timestamp for later review.
